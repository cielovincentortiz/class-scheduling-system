# Class Schedule Viewer

Welcome to the Class Schedule Viewer, the school portal for checking class timetables, managing academic scheduling information, and communicating about class-related concerns.

This guide explains how to use the portal as an administrator, teacher, or student.

## Getting started

1. Open the Class Schedule Viewer website using the link provided by your school.
2. Enter your school email address and password.
3. Select **Sign in**.
4. Your dashboard and available navigation options depend on your account role. The portal header adapts to your screen. Navigation links remain on a single row. If all pages fit, they are shown directly; if space is limited, the remaining pages appear under **More**. On a computer, point to **More** to open it. On a phone or tablet, tap **More**. Select a page to open it; press **Escape** or click elsewhere to close the menu.

Accounts are issued by the school. Contact your school administrator if you do not have an account, cannot sign in, or need your password changed.

## Adjusting text size

The portal includes text-size controls in the top navigation area.

- **A−** makes text smaller.
- **A+** makes text larger.
- The percentage between the buttons shows the current size.
- **Reset** returns text to the standard size.

Text size can be adjusted from 90% to 140%. Your preference is remembered by the same browser on the same device. Changing the setting does not change the timetable or other school records. Use the **Dark / Light** control to switch the portal's appearance. The selected appearance is also remembered on that browser and device.

## Your account menu

Select your name or profile area in the top-right corner to open your account menu. Depending on your screen and device, the menu opens when you point to the profile area, focus it with a keyboard, or select it.

Use **Account details** to review the account information available to you. Use **Sign out** when you finish using a shared computer.

## Administrator guide

Administrators manage school records, schedules, accounts, and Community discussions.

### Overview

The Overview page summarizes the current academic scheduling information and provides shortcuts to common tasks.

### Records

Open **Records** to manage the school's information:

- **Teachers:** maintain teacher details, subjects they can teach, teaching-load limits, availability, and preferences.
- **Programs:** maintain academic programs and year levels.
- **Sections:** maintain section codes, enrolment counts, curriculum assignments, and laboratory batch sizes.
- **Subjects:** maintain subject codes, names, units, classifications, weekly hours, and lecture or laboratory meetings.
- **Rooms:** maintain lecture rooms, computer laboratories, the gym, online venues, and other facilities.
- **Students:** maintain student details, assigned sections, and additional class assignments when applicable.

Add or update the related school record before creating a teacher or student login account.

### Class Schedule

Use **Class Schedule** to review the timetable. Use the available filters to narrow the view by day, teacher, section, or room. Open a schedule entry to review its class details.

### Schedule Generator

Use **Schedule Generator** to create a proposed timetable from the records and scheduling rules entered in the portal.

1. Check that teachers, subjects, sections, rooms, availability, and teaching loads are up to date.
2. Open the generator and select the academic period and generation options shown.
3. Start generation.
4. Review the proposed timetable, unscheduled meetings, and any explanations shown.
5. Make manual adjustments when needed.
6. Run the conflict check before approving the timetable.

The generator may report meetings that it could not place. Review those items and their explanations; do not assume that an incomplete proposal is an approved timetable.

### Consultation

Use **Consultation** to review or manage teacher consultation periods. Reserved consultation periods are treated as unavailable for ordinary class scheduling.

### Reports

Use **Reports** to review scheduling summaries, teaching loads, room use, conflicts, and unscheduled meetings.

### User Accounts

Use **User Accounts** to create, update, deactivate, or remove portal accounts.

- Create the teacher or student record first.
- Open **User Accounts** and create an account for that person.
- Select the correct role and link the account to the matching teacher or student record.
- Give the initial password to the account owner privately.
- Ask the user to sign in and change or protect their password according to school policy.

Never share passwords in Community posts or public messages.

### Settings and data tools

Use **Settings** to review the academic period and scheduling preferences available to administrators. Where enabled, the page also provides backup, restore, and spreadsheet import/export tools. Review a backup before restoring it because a restore may replace current academic records.

## Teacher guide

Teachers can use the portal to review their teaching assignments and communicate with students and administrators.

- **My Schedule:** view assigned class meetings, dates, times, and venues.
- **Teaching Load:** review teaching hours and assigned classes.
- **Consultation:** review consultation availability or reserved consultation periods shown for the account.
- **Community:** post class announcements, class updates, timetable questions, and room or online-class concerns; reply to relevant discussions.

If a timetable entry appears incorrect, create a Community discussion or contact the school administrator. A Community post does not directly change the approved timetable.

## Student guide

Students can use the portal to check their class information and raise class-related concerns.

- **My Schedule:** view the class meetings assigned to your account.
- **My Subjects:** review subjects connected to your class assignments.
- **Community:** ask timetable questions, request a schedule review, report a room or online-class issue, and reply to discussions.

Students with additional class assignments should check all meetings displayed in **My Schedule**. Contact the school administrator if a class you are expected to attend is missing.

## Community guide

Community is a shared discussion space for active portal users. It helps students, teachers, and administrators discuss matters related to classes and schedules.

### Create a post

1. Open **Community**.
2. Select the post category that best matches your topic.
3. Enter a clear title.
4. Explain the concern or announcement in the message field.
5. Select the relevant section when the form provides that option.
6. Review your message and publish it.

Available categories include:

- **Schedule change request:** ask the administrator to review a class day or time.
- **Timetable question:** ask for clarification about a scheduled class.
- **Announcement:** share an important class-related announcement.
- **Class update:** share an update that affects a class.
- **Room / online class issue:** report a venue or synchronous online-class concern.
- **General discussion:** discuss another class-related topic.

### Reply to a post

Open the discussion, read the message and existing replies, enter your response, and submit it. Keep replies relevant and respectful.

### Discussion status

Administrators may update a discussion's status:

- **Open:** the discussion is awaiting review or a response.
- **In review:** the concern is being checked.
- **Resolved:** the issue has been addressed.
- **Closed:** the discussion is no longer active.

Authors can manage their own posts and comments as allowed by the portal. Administrators can moderate discussions and remove content that is inappropriate or unrelated to school matters.

### Important Community rules

- Be respectful and use clear, appropriate language.
- Do not publish passwords, private contact details, grades, sensitive student information, or personal documents.
- Include the subject, section, day, and time when they help explain a timetable concern. Do not include information about other students that they have not agreed to share.
- A schedule-change request is a request for review only. It does not automatically change the official timetable. The administrator must review and update the schedule separately when a change is approved.
- Community discussions are visible to active signed-in portal members. Do not treat a post as a private message.

## Viewing and printing schedules

Use the schedule filters to find the timetable you need. Depending on your role, you may have access to a personal teacher schedule, a section schedule, a room schedule, or the master schedule.

Use the available print option to print a timetable. In the browser's print window, you can choose a connected printer or select **Save as PDF** when available.

## Display preferences

Use the A− and A+ controls in the top navigation to make text comfortable to read. Select **Reset** to return to the standard text size. These display preferences do not change school records.

## If something does not look right

- **You cannot sign in:** check that you are using the school email and password issued to you. Contact the administrator if access is still denied.
- **A schedule is missing:** contact the administrator or raise a Community timetable question. Confirm that you are viewing the correct academic period.
- **A room or time looks incorrect:** report the issue through Community with the subject, section, day, and time.
- **A page does not show the latest information:** refresh the page and sign in again if your session has expired.
- **A button is unavailable:** your account role may not have permission to use that function. Contact the administrator if you believe you need access.

Thank you for using the Class Schedule Viewer.
